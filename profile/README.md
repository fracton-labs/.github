# 🏦 Fracton Labs

### Trustless, on-chain access to real-world yield — built on Stellar & Soroban.

We build permissionless infrastructure that lets anyone, anywhere, earn proportional daily yield from tokenized real-world assets (RWAs) like U.S. Treasuries and money market funds — without minimums, custodians, or gatekeepers.

---

## 🚀 What We're Building

**[Fractionalized Treasury Yield Aggregator (FTYA)](https://github.com/fracton-labs/Fractionalized-Treasury-Yield-Aggregator)**
A non-custodial, on-chain money market fund. Deposit stablecoins, receive fTYA pool shares, and earn daily yield automatically distributed by Soroban smart contracts — sourced from tokenized bonds like Franklin Templeton's BENJI fund.

- 🔓 Permissionless deposits, no KYC required by default
- 📊 Fully transparent, on-chain NAV
- ⚡ Automated daily yield distribution — no manual claiming
- 🧩 Composable fTYA share tokens (SEP-0041 standard)

---

## 🌍 Why Stellar

Low fees, fast finality, native USDC, and a growing ecosystem of tokenized real-world assets make Stellar and Soroban the right foundation for yield infrastructure that needs to work at micro-deposit scale, for everyone, everywhere.
